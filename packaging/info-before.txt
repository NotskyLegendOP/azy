Azy Skin - what this installer does (and does not do)

WHAT IT INSTALLS
  A single small application in its own folder, plus your choice of:
    - starting Azy Skin with Windows (a single per-user registry entry), and
    - applying the Azy dark theme to Premiere Pro automatically.

WHAT IT DOES NOT TOUCH
  - Adobe Premiere Pro, any other Adobe application, or any file inside their
    installation folders.
  - Premiere Pro preferences, projects, caches, plugins or media.
  - Any Adobe resource, executable or library.

  Azy Skin does not use UXP, CEP, ExtendScript or any Premiere API. It does not
  inject code into Premiere, does not modify Premiere's memory or files, and does
  not change how Premiere works. It reads public Windows information about the
  Premiere window and applies documented Windows window-composition settings to
  it - the same mechanism Windows itself uses for dark title bars.

WHAT IT NEEDS
  Windows 10 version 1809 (build 17763) or newer, and Premiere Pro 2024, 2025 or
  2026 for the full treatment. Older or newer builds simply get a more
  conservative version of the theme, never an unstable one.

UNINSTALLING
  The uninstaller removes Azy's files, its settings, its log and its startup
  entry. Premiere Pro is left exactly as it was - it will simply go back to its
  original look.
